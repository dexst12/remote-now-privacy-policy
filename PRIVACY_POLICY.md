# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 16, 2026**

Thank you for using **RemoteNow** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please stop using the App immediately.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use RemoteNow. The App operates primarily as a local network tool.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Local Network & Device Data**
- Your device scans your local Wi-Fi network to discover compatible Smart TVs and streaming devices (e.g., Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, Philips, Hisense, Panasonic).
- IP addresses and device names of TVs on your local network are used solely to establish a connection and are never transmitted to our servers.
- Connection preferences (e.g., last connected device) are stored locally on your device.

**Usage Analytics**
- We use **Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymized, aggregated usage data such as app sessions, feature interactions, and crash reports. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.
- This helps us improve app stability and user experience.

**Device Information**
- We may collect basic device information (device model, OS version) for diagnostic and analytics purposes.

### 1.3 Media Access
- When you use the **Cast** feature, the App accesses photos, videos, and audio files from your device's local storage. This media is streamed directly to your TV over your local Wi-Fi network.
- Media files are **never uploaded to our servers**.

### 1.4 Microphone
- The App requests microphone access solely to enable the **Voice Command** feature, which converts your speech to text to control your TV.
- Audio is processed on-device using the platform's speech recognition service. We do not store or transmit your voice data.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **TV Discovery** | Scanning your local network to find compatible Smart TVs |
| **Remote Control** | Sending commands to your TV over your local Wi-Fi |
| **Media Casting** | Streaming selected media from your device to your TV |
| **Voice Commands** | Converting speech to text for TV control |
| **App Improvement** | Using anonymized analytics to fix bugs and improve features |
| **Preferences** | Remembering your last connected device and in-app settings |

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

---

## 3. Data Storage

- All connection preferences and settings are stored **locally on your device** using secure, platform-provided storage (iOS Keychain / Android Secure Storage).
- We do not maintain any server-side database of user information.
- Anonymized analytics data is stored by **Google Firebase** in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Local Network Access** | Discover and communicate with Smart TVs on your Wi-Fi |
| **Wi-Fi Information** | Identify the network for TV discovery |
| **Microphone** | Voice command input for TV control |
| **Photo Library** | Browse and cast photos/videos to your TV |

You may revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Revoking a permission will disable the corresponding feature.

## 6. Children's Privacy

RemoteNow is not directed at children under the age of **13** (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:
- Local data is stored using platform-level secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore).
- All TV communication occurs over your private, local Wi-Fi network.
- We do not transmit sensitive data over the internet.

Despite these measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your data, including:
- **Access**: Request information about data collected about you.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal data.
- **Opt-Out**: Disable analytics collection (by revoking analytics permissions or uninstalling the App).

Since we collect minimal data and do not operate user accounts, most data rights are automatically satisfied. For analytics data held by Firebase, please refer to [Google's data deletion tools](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190).
