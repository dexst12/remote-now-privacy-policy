# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 6, 2026**

Thank you for using **Remotiq** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please stop using the App immediately.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use Remotiq. The App operates primarily as a local network tool.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

#### Local Network & Device Data

* Your device scans your local Wi-Fi network to discover compatible Smart TVs and streaming devices (including Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, Philips, Hisense, Panasonic, and other supported devices).
* IP addresses and device names of TVs on your local network are used solely to establish a connection and are never transmitted to our servers.
* Connection preferences (such as your last connected device) are stored locally on your device.

#### Usage Analytics

* We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized, aggregated usage data such as app sessions, feature interactions, performance metrics, and crash reports.
* This information cannot reasonably be used to identify you personally and helps us improve app stability, performance, and user experience.

#### Device Information

* We may collect basic device information, including device model, operating system version, and application version, for diagnostic and analytics purposes.

### 1.3 Media Access

* When you use the **Cast** feature, the App accesses photos, videos, and audio files stored on your device.
* Selected media is streamed directly to your TV over your local Wi-Fi network.
* Media files are never uploaded to our servers.

### 1.4 Microphone

* The App requests microphone access solely to enable the **Voice Command** feature.
* Audio is processed using your device's built-in speech recognition services or platform-provided speech processing.
* We do not store, record, or transmit your voice recordings to our servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

| Purpose         | Description                                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| TV Discovery    | Scanning your local network to find compatible Smart TVs and streaming devices |
| Remote Control  | Sending commands to connected devices over your local Wi-Fi network            |
| Media Casting   | Streaming selected media from your device to your TV                           |
| Voice Commands  | Converting speech input into remote control actions                            |
| App Improvement | Using anonymized analytics to improve stability, performance, and features     |
| Preferences     | Remembering connected devices and user settings locally                        |

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.
