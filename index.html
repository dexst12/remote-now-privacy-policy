Thank you for using Remotiq ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please stop using the App immediately.

01

Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use Remotiq. The App operates primarily as a local network tool.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Local Network & Device Data

Your device scans your local Wi-Fi network to discover compatible Smart TVs and streaming devices (e.g., Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, Philips, Hisense, Panasonic). IP addresses and device names of TVs on your local network are used solely to establish a connection and are never transmitted to our servers. Connection preferences (e.g., last connected device) are stored locally on your device.

Usage Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized, aggregated usage data such as app sessions, feature interactions, and crash reports. This data cannot be used to identify you personally. This helps us improve app stability and user experience.

Device Information

We may collect basic device information (device model, OS version) for diagnostic and analytics purposes.

1.3 Media Access

When you use the Cast feature, the App accesses photos, videos, and audio files from your device's local storage. This media is streamed directly to your TV over your local Wi-Fi network. Media files are never uploaded to our servers.

1.4 Microphone

The App requests microphone access solely to enable the Voice Command feature, which converts your speech to text to control your TV. Audio is processed on-device using the platform's speech recognition service. We do not store or transmit your voice data.

Data Storage

All connection preferences and settings are stored locally on your device using secure, platform-provided storage (iOS Keychain / Android Secure Storage). We do not maintain any server-side database of user information. Anonymized analytics data is stored by Google Firebase in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
