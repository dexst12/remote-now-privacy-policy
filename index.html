Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Remotiq ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Remotiq mobile application, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Remotiq is designed to function without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

We do not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Payment information
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Audio recordings
  • Browsing history
  • Search history

Diagnostic and Performance Data

To improve app stability, reliability, and performance, Remotiq uses Google Firebase Crashlytics.

Crashlytics may collect diagnostic information such as:

  • Crash reports
  • Error logs
  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Performance and stability metrics

This information is used solely for:

  • Diagnosing and fixing crashes
  • Improving app performance
  • Monitoring application stability
  • Enhancing overall user experience

Analytics

Remotiq may use limited analytics and diagnostic information to understand app performance and feature usage. Any analytics information is used only in aggregated or pseudonymous form to improve the application.

We do not use analytics data to identify individual users.

Tracking

Remotiq does not track users across third-party apps, websites, or services for advertising purposes.

Remotiq does not sell personal information and does not share user data with data brokers.

Data collected through Firebase services is used solely for diagnostics, analytics, app functionality, and product improvement.

App Tracking Transparency

Because Remotiq does not perform tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, the app does not request permission to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Local Network Access

Remotiq may request access to devices on your local network to discover and communicate with compatible TVs and streaming devices.

Local network communication occurs only to enable remote-control functionality and device discovery. Information discovered on your local network is not sold or used for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

Remotiq may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting and diagnostics)
  • Firebase Analytics (application analytics, if enabled)

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the app against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Children's Privacy

Remotiq is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or restriction of data processing. You may contact us to exercise any applicable rights.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
