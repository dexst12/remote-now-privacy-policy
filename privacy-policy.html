# Privacy Policy **Last Updated: June 6, 2026** Thank you for using **Remotiq** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please discontinue use of the App. --- # 1. Information We Collect ## 1.1 Information You Provide Remotiq does not require user registration or account creation. You can use the App without providing personal information. If you contact us for support, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message. --- ## 1.2 Automatically Collected Information ### Local Network & Device Data Remotiq uses your local network to discover and connect to compatible Smart TVs and streaming devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network. * Device discovery and communication occur primarily within your local Wi-Fi network. * IP addresses and device names are used only to establish connections with compatible devices. * This information is not transmitted to our servers. * Connection preferences, such as previously connected devices, are stored locally on your device. ### Usage Analytics We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized and aggregated information about how the App is used, including: * App sessions * Feature usage * Performance metrics * Device type * Operating system version This information helps us improve functionality, reliability, and user experience. ### Crash Diagnostics We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information when the App encounters unexpected errors. Crash reports may include: * Device model * Operating system version * App version * Diagnostic logs related to the crash This information is used solely to identify and fix technical issues. ### Device Information We may collect limited technical information about your device, including: * Device model * Operating system version * Application version * Language settings This information is used for diagnostics, compatibility, and performance improvements. --- ## 1.3 Media Access When you use casting features, Remotiq may access photos, videos, and audio files stored on your device. * Media is streamed directly from your device to compatible TVs or streaming devices over your local Wi-Fi network. * Media files are never uploaded to our servers. * Access occurs only when you choose media to cast. --- ## 1.4 Microphone Access Remotiq requests microphone access solely to enable voice command and voice search functionality. * Audio is processed using platform-provided speech recognition services. * We do not store voice recordings. * We do not transmit voice recordings to our servers. You may disable microphone access at any time through your device settings. --- # 2.
