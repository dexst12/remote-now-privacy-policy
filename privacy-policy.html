Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

Thank you for using Remotiq ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Remotiq does not require user registration or account creation. You can use the App without providing personal information.

If you contact us for support, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Local Network & Device Data

  • Device discovery and communication occur primarily within your local Wi-Fi network.
  • IP addresses and device names are used only to establish connections with compatible devices.
  • This information is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Connection preferences, such as previously connected devices, are stored locally on your device.

Usage Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized and aggregated information about how the App is used.

Crash Diagnostics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information.

1.3 Media Access

When you use casting features, Remotiq may access photos, videos, and audio files stored on your device. Media is streamed directly from your device to compatible TVs over your local Wi-Fi network. Media files are never uploaded to our servers.

1.4 Microphone Access

Remotiq requests microphone access solely to enable voice command and voice search functionality. Audio is processed using platform-provided speech recognition services. We do not store or transmit voice recordings.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Remotiq does not collect, store, or process:

User accounts
Passwords
Precise location
Contacts
Calendars
Messages
Browsing history
Payment information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. How We Use Information

Purpose Description
Device DiscoveryDiscover compatible Smart TVs and streaming devices
Remote ControlSend commands to connected devices
Media CastingStream selected content to connected devices
Voice CommandsEnable voice-based controls
App ImprovementImprove performance, reliability, and features
DiagnosticsDetect, investigate, and fix crashes

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Device settings and connection preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not maintain user accounts or a server-side user database.
  • Analytics and diagnostic information may be processed by Google Firebase.
  • Local data remains on your device until you remove the App or clear its data.

5. International Data Transfers

Third-party providers used by Remotiq, including Google Firebase, may process information on servers located outside your country. Such transfers are performed in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

6. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose
Firebase AnalyticsUsage analytics
Firebase CrashlyticsCrash diagnostics
Google FontsTypography rendering
Apple / Android Speech ServicesVoice recognition

7. Permissions

Permission Purpose
Local Network AccessDiscover and communicate with devices
MicrophoneVoice commands and search
Photo Library / Media FilesSelect media for casting
NotificationsOptional connection updates

8. Push Notifications

Notifications are optional and can be disabled in device settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Remotiq is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Data Security

We implement reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Many rights are supported by design due to minimal data collection.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact Us
